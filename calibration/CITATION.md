# Citation

The dataset page asks users to cite:

> Mills, M. O., & Ghorbani, A. A. (2025). Beyond rules: Behavioral anomaly detection for post-quantum cryptography operational assurance in TLS. *Journal of Information Security and Applications* (under review).

Also cite the dataset page itself, with access date:

> Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity. (2025). *PQC-OAV-dataset-2025*. University of New Brunswick. https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/pqc-oav-dataset-2025.html (accessed DD Month 2026)

Before submission: check if the paper has been published and update the reference.
